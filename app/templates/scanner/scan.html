Email Phishing Defense & Mail Recommendations
INBOX PROTECTION
Inspect Hyperlinks First

Hover over email buttons before clicking. Verify the target domain matches the official organization rather than lookalike subdomains.

Check Sender Headers

Beware of spoofed display names. Check whether the sender domain passes SPF/DKIM authentication and does not use mismatched TLDs.

Urgency & Credential Bait

Phishing emails create false urgency ("Account locked in 24 hours"). Never input passwords directly via links received in unverified emails.

Safe Risk 0–29

Valid SSL certificates, aged domain registration, natural token entropy, and clean DOM structure.

Suspicious Risk 30–59

Unusual redirect chains, young domain age, brand keyword variations, or unverified credential forms.

Phishing Risk 60–100

Direct IP hosting, spoofed brand domains, credential phishing forms, hidden iframes, or malicious redirects.
