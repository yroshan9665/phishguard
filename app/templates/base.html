Platform Guidance, Scoring Metrics, and Incident Response

The risk score combines the output probability of our Random Forest ML model with 15 structural and environmental heuristics:
  • 0 to 29 (SAFE): Valid SSL, established domain age (>6 months), clean syntactic structure, and legitimate anchor distributions.
  • 30 to 69 (SUSPICIOUS): High entropy, missing/untrusted SSL certificate, redirection chains, brand keywords in subdomains, or freshly registered domain.
  • 70 to 100 (PHISHING): Direct IP address hosting, credential harvesting path keywords, deceptive @ symbol obfuscation, or multiple high-severity heuristics triggered.

After inspecting any link on the Scanner page, navigate to the scan result and click the "Export Forensic PDF" button. The platform compiles an official, downloadable security audit detailing all 15 heuristic scores, SSL telemetry status, whois age, and recommended mitigation actions.

On the login screen, click the "Switch to Admin Portal ↻" button located at the bottom of the sign-in card. The card will execute a 3D flip animation to reveal the administrator authentication interface.

Do not click any embedded links or download attachments. Copy the link address (right-click → "Copy Link Address"), paste it into the Phish Gaurd AI Scanner, and inspect the risk score and heuristic breakdown before taking any further action.

Our feature extraction engine analyzes: URL length, domain length, path length, dot count, hyphen count, underscore count, slash count, question mark count, equals count, at-symbol presence, percentage symbol presence, IP address format, HTTPS protocol, sensitive keyword presence, and subdomain depth.
